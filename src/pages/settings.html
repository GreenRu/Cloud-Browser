Search shortcuts

Type the keyword, a space, then your search — gt bonjour opens Google Translate, yt lofi searches YouTube. In your own templates, %s is where the query is inserted.

Bring things over

Bookmarks can be read straight out of another browser and kept here as droplets. Passwords cannot: they are sealed to the browser that saved them, so export them from there and read the file here — which is the door every browser leaves open for it.
